Dangling Modifier

เคสประจำกลุ่มผ่าตัด (Case Spotlight)

"Walking down the street, the trees were beautiful."

Diagnosis Hints:

The participial phrase "Walking down the street" lacks a logical subject. Trees cannot walk down the street!

Goal Structure:

Reconstruct sentence so the modifier clearly modifies the correct noun (e.g. "As I was walking down the street, the trees were beautiful.").
